## Voice rules (for anything drafted in Romil's voice)

No em-dashes. No arrows. No parentheticals-as-asides. No "I'm passionate about". No "I hope this finds you well". Direct declarative sentences, US spelling, colons to introduce lists. No aphorisms, no rhetorical inversions. Avoid the words "shape", "wheelhouse", "load-bearing".
